# Events to Log

### Instruments to pay attention to:

- OBS
- magnetometer
- sub-bottom profiler (SBP)
- gravitometer
- multibeam

## OBS events

<!-- > [!IMPORTANT]
> OBS EVENTS ARE THE MOST IMPORTANT THINGS TO LOG. -->

- **OBS deployed**
  - When the OBS release is pulled/the OBS hits the water
  - Need to log the DEPTH at the time of deployment
- **OBS on bottom**
- **OBS survey start/end**
  - AKA when we are navigating around the OBS to locate its position on the seafloor

## Other

- magnetometer survey start/end
- when we pass waypoints
- when we turn
- changes in settings to the sub-bottom profiler, etc. (optional?)
